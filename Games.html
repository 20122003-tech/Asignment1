My top 3 games

My three favourite games

Valorant

Valorant is a free-to-play 5v5 tactical shooter from Riot Games. Teams take turns attacking and defending, and every player picks an agent with their own abilities.

  • Rounds are played as attackers against defenders
  • Each agent has a unique set of abilities
  • Free to play on PC and console

Why I like it: I love Valorant as playing its competitive game mode gives me a sense of reward whenever I win or gain a new rank. I play this game with other SETU students on a Monday evening with the SETU E-sports club.

Read more about Valorant on Wikipedia

Cyberpunk 2077

An open-world action RPG set in Night City, a huge futuristic city in California. You play a character named V, at the beginning you have a choice to start as either a nomad in the wasteland on the edges of the city looting anything you can, Street kid where you are a mercenary looking for a way to survive in Night City going gig to gig, or as a low-level corporate worker for the major company Arasaka.

  • Mostly played from a first-person view
  • Your choices in quests and dialogue change how the story plays out
  • Made by the studio behind The Witcher 3

Why I like it: I love Cyberpunk as its story gets you immersed inside Night City, the availability of customization is what makes this game so fun to play as finding new ways to play the game or the idea that whatever dialogue option you choose, affects the outcome of the story.

Read more about Cyberpunk 2077 on Wikipedia

Dishonored

A first-person stealth action game set in the city of Dunwall where the streets are ridden with the plague. You play as Corvo Attano, a royal bodyguard framed for murder who gains supernatural powers where he uses them to seek revenge on those who conspired against him.

  • You can sneak past enemies or fight your way through
  • Powers like Blink let you teleport short distances
  • How you play changes how the story ends

Why I like it: I love to comeback to playing this game as it doesn't take long to complete the story. Similar to Cyberpunk the choices you make effect the story, the game is based on being stealthy though you can use any ammount of combat you want. I love the level design as there is always different ways to sneak around each time you play.
